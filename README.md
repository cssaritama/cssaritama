### What I build

I work at the intersection of **AI engineering, data systems, product architecture and decision support**. My projects are designed as end-to-end systems rather than isolated notebooks: ingestion, retrieval, models/agents, evaluation, APIs or interfaces, observability, reproducibility and deployment architecture.

My current focus is on:

- **Agentic AI systems** with tools, evidence boundaries and human accountability;
- **Decision Intelligence** that connects structured data, unstructured knowledge and real decisions;
- **RAG and retrieval evaluation**, including hybrid search, reranking and grounded generation;
- **production ML / MLOps**, monitoring and reproducible pipelines;
- **cloud-native AI architecture**, with a growing focus on Google Cloud and Vertex AI;

## How I approach AI systems

```text
Business / decision problem
          ↓
Data + knowledge boundaries
          ↓
Deterministic tools + ML / LLM components
          ↓
Evaluation before confidence
          ↓
Human decision / workflow
          ↓
Observability + feedback
          ↓
Learning + system improvement
```

I am especially interested in environments where AI must operate with **evidence, uncertainty, security, governance and measurable business impact**.

## Working principles

- Build systems that can be **run and reviewed**, not only presented.
- Keep deterministic calculations outside the LLM when code can do them better.
- Treat **evaluation, observability and abstention** as product features.
- Separate what is implemented from what is roadmap.
- Prefer architectures that can evolve from open research prototypes to secure enterprise systems.
- Make AI useful to human judgment rather than hiding uncertainty behind confident language.
